The next wave of AI is about permission.

As agents move from answering questions to taking action, the most useful question may be: what have you allowed?

Illustrative article · AI × Crypto · 6 min read

A chatbot can suggest a purchase. An agent with access to a wallet can take the next step. That difference puts a familiar question at the center of a new interface: who has permission to do what?

Start with the permission, not the promise.

For a reader trying a new tool, the distinction should be easy to see. What can it read? What can it change? Can it spend money? And can you stop it without navigating a maze of settings?
