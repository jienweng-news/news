# Token on Air visual direction

Status: founder selected the Persona-inspired newsroom concept on 2026-10-22 after comparing it with Editorial × graphic. Application implementation remains pending.

## Confirmed direction

Use an original red, black and warm-white graphic identity: cutout-style masthead, condensed display typography, angled section treatments and a dark latest-headlines column. Preserve the demonstrated news hierarchy: lead story, latest headlines, AI coverage, crypto coverage and deeper analysis. The reference borrows visual energy from Persona 5; no game artwork, characters or logos are used.

Keep long-form body text upright, high contrast and comfortable to read. Carry the identity through article titles and wayfinding without extending diagonal treatments into running text. Maintain semantic navigation, keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior and responsive reading widths. English and Traditional Chinese must both fit the design; editorial prominence is not based only on color.

## Brand

- Name: Token on Air, shared by both language editions.
